Retail · Profitability

Global Superstore: where profit is lost

$12.64M in sales but only $1.47M profit. 30% of orders lose money, and orders discounted over 30% run at a -51% margin. I built a 4-page Power BI report to show where and why.

Retail · Revenue

Vrinda Store: why revenue fell 20%

Revenue dropped from Rs.1.83M in March to Rs.1.46M by November. I traced most of the drop to female customers on Amazon and Myntra and estimated a Rs.2M recovery from a targeted campaign.

SQL · Excel · Power BI
E-commerce · Customer retention

Olist: why 97% of customers never buy again

I analysed 93,358 customers with SQL. Repeat buyers spend 92% more, and those who return take about 81 days, so I recommended a reactivation campaign around day 55 to 65.

MySQL · Python · Power BI
